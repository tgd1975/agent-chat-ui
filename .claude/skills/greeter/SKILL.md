# Greeter

A small skill that produces warm, personalized greetings.

When invoked:

1. If the person's **name** or preferred **language** is not given, ask for them
   (one short question).
2. Produce a **2-line** greeting in the requested language and tone.
3. Keep it warm, friendly, and concise — no more than two lines.

## Examples

- "Greet Mia in Austrian German, cheerful" -> a short, cheerful 2-line greeting.
- "Welcome a new teammate named Sam" -> a warm 2-line welcome.
